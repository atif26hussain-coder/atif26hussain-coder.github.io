Basketball Shootout 3D

A 3D three-point shootout basketball game for Android. Play offline, beat the buzzer and become the shootout champion.

Privacy

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, contacts or location. Game progress is stored only on your device.

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers to show ads and measure them. See how Google uses data in ads. You can choose your ad consent options inside the game.

Contact

For questions or support, use the contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
